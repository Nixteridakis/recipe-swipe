Spicy Garlic Shrimp Pasta

schedule Prep: 10 mins
skillet Cook: 15 mins
group Serves: 2 persons

Ingredients

  • check
    250g Linguine or Spaghetti
  • 450g Large Shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 6 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp Red pepper flakes (adjust for spice)
  • 1/2 cup Dry white wine (or chicken broth)
  • 1/4 cup Fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
  • Juice of half a lemon

Chef's Note

"Don't overcook the shrimp. They only need about 2 minutes per side until they turn pink and opaque."

lightbulb

Preparation Steps

1

Boil the Pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook according to package instructions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup of pasta water before draining.

2

Sauté Aromatics

In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and red pepper flakes. Sauté for about 1 minute until the garlic is golden and fragrant, but not burnt.

3

Cook the Shrimp

Increase heat to medium-high. Add the shrimp to the skillet in a single layer. Season with salt and pepper. Cook for 2 minutes per side until pink and cooked through. Remove shrimp and set aside.

4

Deglaze and Emulsify

Pour the white wine into the skillet, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Let it simmer for 2 minutes until reduced by half. Stir in the lemon juice and reserved pasta water.

5

The Final Toss

Add the cooked pasta and shrimp back into the skillet. Toss everything together for 1 minute so the sauce coats the pasta beautifully. Garnish generously with fresh parsley.
